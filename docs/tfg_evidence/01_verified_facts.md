> **Read this before F02–F05.** The values labelled "stock" in F02–F05 are the *shipped* RT-Thread configuration. They are **not** the Phase 1 configuration. Phase 1 pins `TCP_SND_QUEUELEN` = 44 and `MEMP_NUM_TCP_SEG` = 44 across every build, and varies `TCP_SND_BUF` over the ladder L0 = 8196 / L1 = 16384 / L2 = 32768. The stock values survive only as the `STOCK` arm of the pinning control. See `00_project_state.md` and `08_design_rationale.md`. Effective per-configuration values are archived in `raw_logs/macro_dump_{L0,L1,L2,STOCK}.txt`.
